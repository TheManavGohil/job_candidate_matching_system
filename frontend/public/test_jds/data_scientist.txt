# Machine Learning Engineer / Data Scientist

## About the Role
Join our AI team to develop and deploy machine learning models that power our candidate matching engine. You will work on NLP tasks, vector embeddings, and ranking algorithms.

## Required Skills
- Strong proficiency in Python and ML libraries (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn)
- Deep understanding of NLP (Transformers, BERT, LLMs, LangChain)
- Experience with Vector Databases (Qdrant, Pinecone, or Milvus)
- Solid foundation in statistics and linear algebra
- Experience with data processing libraries (Pandas, NumPy)
- Familiarity with deploying ML models in production (BentoML, Ray Serve, or FastAPI)

## Qualifications
- MS or PhD in Computer Science, Math, or a related quantitative field
- 3+ years of experience building and deploying ML models
- Experience working with large-scale datasets

## Responsibilities
- Develop and optimize NLP models for semantic search and ranking
- Implement and experiment with state-of-the-art embedding techniques
- Build data pipelines for training and evaluating models
- Collaborate with the product team to define and implement new AI features
- Stay up-to-date with latest research in NLP and Information Retrieval
